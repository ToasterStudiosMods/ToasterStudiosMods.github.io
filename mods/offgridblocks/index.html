Off da grid blocks.

A wand that places full, working, hitboxed blocks at any angle, free of the grid. Based on that ElementX video where blocks sit on an offset. Now you can actually do it.

What it is.

Minecraft puts every block on a tidy grid. This mod hands you a wand that ignores it. Point, place, and the block lands on an offset, at an angle, wherever you aimed, with a full working hitbox you can walk into and break like any other block.

How it works.

Grab the offgrid wand. Press B to open the block picker, choose what you want to place, then use the wand to drop it off the grid. Breaking is normal: just mine the block like you would any other. Breaking speed isn't the point here, the placement is.

Controls.

  • B Open the block picker menu
  • Right click Place a block off the grid
  • Left click Destroy a placed block
