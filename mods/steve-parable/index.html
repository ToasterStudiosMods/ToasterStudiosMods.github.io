What it is.

The Stanley Parable, but the office is a survival world and Stanley is you. A narrator watches the whole run and reacts to what you actually do — first tree punched, first ore mined, first thing crafted, the mob you killed, the death you earned, the long stretch where you stood still and did nothing at all. It uses your actual Minecraft username, so the commentary lands a little too personally.

It runs offline.

The voice is real neural text-to-speech synthesized on your machine with sherpa-onnx — no API keys, no per-line bill, no server to phone home to. Download the model once and it works on a plane. Lines are cached as audio so you're not re-synthesizing the same quip every time you punch a tree.

Two voices.

Pick Alan (Piper) when you want fast and reactive, or Kokoro when you want it to sound nicer and don't mind a beat of delay. Captions ride along the bottom of the screen in sync with the audio, so you catch every line even with the volume down.

The cursed bits.

  • 550+ unique narrator lines across mining, crafting, death, mobs, biomes, dimensions and idling
  • Title a Stanley Parable-style menu, office and all, plus an intro cutscene on new worlds
  • Permadeath FORCED mode that auto-deletes the world when you die. The narrator approves.

Controls.

  • N Open voice & narrator settings
  • Esc Same settings, from the pause menu
