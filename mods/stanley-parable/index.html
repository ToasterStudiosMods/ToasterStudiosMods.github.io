What it is.

A cursed mod for The Stanley Parable. You boot the game and the title says The Stanley Parable 55 — Toaster's mod menu — and things go downhill from there in the way they're supposed to. New rooms, , and the Narrator reacting to choices we definitely should not have given you.

Why a Minecraft studio is doing this.

Because the loop is too good to leave alone. The Stanley Parable started life as a free Half-Life 2 mod in 2011 — 90,000 downloads in two weeks, made by one person teaching himself the Source SDK off wikis. Sound familiar? And the 2013 remake literally drops you into a Minecraft level (Notch signed off on it). So a tiny Minecraft mod studio modding The Stanley Parable back isn't a pivot. It's the loop closing.

Where it's at.

The mod menu works, the cat has been briefed, and we're writing more than we should. It's toasting — honest trade-off, not a bug. When it's ready it goes out wherever The Stanley Parable keeps its mods, with the source open like everything else we make. Want a ping? The button's right up there.
