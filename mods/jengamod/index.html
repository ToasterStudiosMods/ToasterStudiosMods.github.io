What it is.

Real physics Jenga, the kind from that one systemzee video. Every piece is its own physics object running on the Sable engine, so the tower actually leans, settles, and topples instead of blocks just popping out of existence. A piece is a 1x3x9 stack of stripped logs, and layers alternate direction just like the real game.

How to play.

  • /stack <N> Spawn a tower in front of you, up to 999 layers
  • Right click Grab a piece (with an empty hand)
  • Scroll Rotate the piece you're holding
  • Right click Let go and watch it settle

What you need.

Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric, with Fabric API and the Sable physics mod in your mods folder. The whole thing is open source.
