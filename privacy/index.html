Privacy Policy.

Plain talk about what this site collects and why. Short version: we don't run accounts or ask for your personal details. The data that does get handled comes from a few third-party tools, listed below.

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Who we are

Toaster Studios ("we", "us") is a small mod studio based in New Jersey, USA, making mods for Minecraft and The Stanley Parable. This policy covers the website at toasterstudiosmods.github.io and any subpages. If you have questions, email toasterstudiosmods@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or submit personal information to use this site. We do not sell your data. The information that is processed comes from the third-party services we use, plus standard technical data your browser sends to any website:

  • Cookies and similar technologies set by Google advertising and the live-chat widget (details below).
  • Technical/log data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, handled by our host and ad/chat providers.
  • A single local preference (your light/dark mode choice) stored on your own device.

Cookies & advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info (or youronlinechoices.eu in the EU).
  • For more on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Live chat (Tawk.to)

We use Tawk.to to provide an optional live-chat widget. If you start a chat, Tawk.to processes the messages you send and may collect technical information (such as your IP address and device/browser details) and set cookies to run the chat. We use this only to reply to you. See the Tawk.to Privacy Policy. If you don't open the chat, you don't need to share anything.

Download statistics (Modrinth)

Some pages show live download counts. These are fetched from Modrinth's public API. This is a one-way read of public numbers - the site does not send your personal information to Modrinth simply because you viewed a page.

Local storage

We store one small setting in your browser's local storage - your light/dark theme preference (key ts-mode). It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it anytime by clearing your browser data.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log standard request data (for example, IP addresses and user agents) to operate and secure the service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, email us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads via the Google and aboutads.info links above.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings (some features, like chat, may stop working).
  • Simply don't open the live chat if you'd rather not share chat data.

Third-party links

We link out to places like Modrinth, GitHub, and YouTube. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we're not responsible for their practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or its tools change. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email toasterstudiosmods@gmail.com.
