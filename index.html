§ 02 / The studio

One modder, one very serious cat.

"Hire me, or else." Toaster, lead creature designer
Fig. 01 / the founder

Toaster Studios is a one-person mod shop with a cat on payroll. We started a few months ago and we ship slow on purpose.

The brief is short: crazy things that unbrain your brain. Physics jokes, tiny mechanics, the occasional regrettable mob — and if a YouTuber showed off a mod and never released it, we'll have a go at recreating it. Mostly in Minecraft. Now also in The Stanley Parable.

Two mods are live so far. A Stanley Parable thing is toasting. The cat supervises.

§ 03 / What we make

A short list, weird on purpose.

01

Cursed mechanics

One small, slightly broken-feeling idea, tuned until it's funny. Fabric for Minecraft, client + server.

02

YouTuber mod recreations

Saw a mod in a video and nobody ever shipped it? Send the link. We'll have a crack at it, that's how Jenga Mod happened.

03

Narrative mischief

The Stanley Parable started life as a mod. We're modding it back — new endings, new doors, a narrator who'd rather we didn't.

04

Regrettable mobs

Tameable, untameable, or actively spiteful. If you have a sketch on a napkin we can probably model around it.

05

Suggestions, seriously

The whole studio is one person and a cat. Open an issue on GitHub, drop a Modrinth comment, whatever, we read them.

§ 04 / How we work

Proof. Mix. Toast. Plate.

01 / Proof

You send the link.

A YouTube clip, a screenshot, a half-finished idea typed at 2am. We write back with what we think it'd take.

02 / Mix

We prototype.

Couple of weekends of trying weird stuff in Fabric. Most of it goes in the bin. We show you the survivors.

03 / Toast

We build it properly.

Code, balance, sounds, permissions, the edge cases. The cat watches. This part is most of the project.

04 / Plate

Out the door.

Minecraft mods go up on Modrinth; the rest go wherever that game keeps its mods. GitHub issues open, replies in the comments. If it breaks, we patch it.
